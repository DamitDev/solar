# S-056: Progress-aware resolver timeouts (replace the total pull bound)

## Description

Follow-up to S-055. The model resolvers wrap their downloads in
`aiohttp.ClientTimeout(total=settings.model_pull_timeout_s)`. A `total`
bound measures wall-clock time for the whole transfer, so it cannot tell a
healthy slow download from a stalled one: a large model on a modest link
is cancelled at 30 minutes even though every byte was arriving on
schedule. Raising `model_pull_timeout_s` trades one arbitrary cutoff for a
larger one and lengthens the time a genuinely wedged transfer holds the
slot.

S-055 makes the resulting failure `recoverable` so the next round retries
and the operator sees an amber notice, but the transfer still restarts
from the beginning at every bound.

## Goal

- Replace the `total` bound in `repo.py` and `huggingface.py` with a
  read-idle bound (`sock_read`), so the timeout measures **silence**
  rather than duration: a transfer that keeps delivering bytes runs to
  completion, and one that stops delivering fails promptly instead of
  after the full budget.
- Keep a generous overall ceiling if one is still wanted, chosen so it
  bounds pathological cases rather than ordinary large downloads.
- Re-check `model_pull_timeout_s`'s documented meaning in
  `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` once the semantics change.

## Additional Notes

The resolvers are shared with paths outside the change that raised this,
which is why it was split out of S-055 rather than done there.

Interaction to preserve: the reconciler's progress-aware wait and the
`pull_progress` freshness check already distinguish "working" from
"wedged" at the control layer. A read-idle bound makes the resolver agree
with that judgement instead of contradicting it.
